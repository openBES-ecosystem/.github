# .github

This repository contains organization-wide community health files and templates for the **openBES-ecosystem**.

## About openBES-ecosystem

openBES-ecosystem is an **open research ecosystem** for **early-stage building energy simulation**, supporting the design and assessment of **net-zero and low-carbon buildings**.

## What's in This Repository

- **profile/README.md**: Organization profile that appears on our GitHub page
- **CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md**: Community standards and expectations
- **CONTRIBUTING.md**: Guidelines for contributing to our projects
- **SECURITY.md**: Security policy and vulnerability reporting
- **.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/**: Issue templates for bugs, features, and research contributions
- **.github/pull_request_template.md**: Pull request template

## About Building Energy Simulation

Building energy simulation helps researchers, architects, and engineers:
- Model and predict building energy consumption
- Optimize building design for energy efficiency
- Assess pathways to net-zero energy buildings
- Reduce operational carbon emissions
- Support evidence-based sustainable design decisions
