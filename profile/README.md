## Welcome to the openBES ecosystem

**openBES-ecosystem** is an open research ecosystem for **early-stage building energy simulation**, supporting transparent, flexible, and reproducible analysis of building energy performance.

The ecosystem supports low-carbon and net-zero building design, particularly during the conceptual and early design stages, where decisions have the largest impact.

---

### 🙋‍♀️ What we do
The openBES ecosystem brings together:
- Open-source tools for early-stage building energy simulation
- Methods that prioritise simplicity, transparency, and rapid exploration
- Academic collaboration across institutions and disciplines

Current core tools include:
- openBES-XL – spreadsheet-based workflows for rapid early-stage analysis
- openBES-py – a Python-based framework for extensible and reproducible modelling

---

### 🌈 Get involved
We welcome contributions from researchers, students, and practitioners.

You can contribute by:
- Reporting issues or suggesting enhancements
- Contributing code, documentation, or examples
- Sharing case studies or validation datasets
- Participating in discussions on methods and assumptions

Please see individual repositories for contribution guidelines.

### 🧙 Guiding principles
- Openness and transparency
- Scientific rigour and reproducibility
- Early-stage decision support, not black-box optimisation
- Academic collaboration and long-term sustainability
