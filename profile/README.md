# Welcome to openBES-ecosystem 🏢⚡

## Open Research Ecosystem for Building Energy Simulation

openBES-ecosystem is an open research platform dedicated to advancing **early-stage building energy simulation** for the design and assessment of **net-zero and low-carbon buildings**.

### 🎯 Our Mission

We provide researchers, architects, engineers, and policymakers with open-source tools and methodologies to:

- **Simulate** building energy performance in the early design stages
- **Assess** pathways to net-zero and low-carbon building operations
- **Support** evidence-based decision-making for sustainable building design
- **Advance** open research practices in building energy modeling

### 🌱 Focus Areas

- **Early-Stage Simulation**: Tools optimized for conceptual and schematic design phases
- **Net-Zero Buildings**: Models and workflows for achieving net-zero energy targets
- **Low-Carbon Assessment**: Life-cycle carbon analysis and operational carbon reduction
- **Open Science**: Transparent, reproducible, and collaborative research methods
